# Redirects

Manage the site's CMS-driven redirects from the editor, and see what visitors actually get.

- **Redirects** view (Extensions in the navigation rail):
  - every rule in the Redirect Config the site reads, with loops, chains, duplicates, dead destinations
    and rules the site doesn't serve yet;
  - **Test a URL**: every hop a visitor goes through, and how the destination answers;
  - add a redirect, and enable or disable one.
- **Redirects** sidebar on a page:
  - the redirects that lead to it, and a warning when a rule makes the page unreachable;
  - earlier URLs of the page (its URL segment changed) with no redirect yet, and a one-click fix.
